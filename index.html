GhostLoader Privacy Policy

Last updated: August 9, 2026

Overview

GhostLoader ("the App") is a file download manager for Android. We are committed to protecting your privacy. This policy explains what data we collect and how we use it.

Data Collection

GhostLoader does not collect, store, or transmit any personal data.

  • No account or registration required
  • No analytics or tracking
  • No advertising SDKs
  • No data shared with third parties
  • No cookies or identifiers collected

Data Stored on Device

The App stores the following data locally on your device only:

  • Download history: URLs, filenames, and download status are saved in the app's local storage (SharedPreferences) so your download list persists between sessions.
  • Downloaded files: Files you download are saved to the app's local storage directory on your device.
  • Preferences: Settings such as your preferred backend URL are stored locally.

This data never leaves your device and is fully deleted when you uninstall the App.

Network Activity

The App connects to the internet solely to:

  • Download files from URLs you provide
  • Resolve URL shorteners and redirects
  • Load web pages in the built-in browser

No data is sent to our servers or any third-party service. The App does not have its own servers.

Permissions

  • Internet: Required to download files and browse the web.
  • Network State: Used to check if you have an internet connection.
  • Notifications: Used to show download progress notifications. You can deny this permission.
  • Foreground Service: Allows downloads to continue when the app is in the background.

Children's Privacy

The App is not directed at children under 13. We do not knowingly collect data from children.

Changes to This Policy

We may update this policy from time to time. Changes will be reflected by updating the "Last updated" date above.
